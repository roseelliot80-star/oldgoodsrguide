About the publication

Practical research for a more useful test drive.

OldGoodsUSR Auto Guide is a working automotive publication focused on clear, source-linked information for U.S. drivers.

What we publish

Our guides translate manufacturer specifications into questions readers can use at a dealership. We pay particular attention to seat adjustment, entry and exit, control reach, visibility aids, parking, liftgate operation, and cargo space with specific seating rows raised.

Our approach is designed to be respectful and useful for drivers aged 45 and older, including retirees, without making assumptions about anyone’s health, income, driving ability, or preferences.

What we do not do

We do not sell vehicles, quote local inventory, promise dealer discounts, or present editorial rankings as road tests. We do not ask readers to register before reading a guide.

Vehicle specifications and prices can change. A manufacturer source supports the research, but the window sticker and an in-person check remain the best way to verify the exact vehicle in front of you.
