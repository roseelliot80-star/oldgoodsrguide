Editorial Methodology

Specific trims. Direct sources. Clear limits.

Our rankings are editorial judgments about everyday usability, not laboratory scores or substitutes for a test drive.

Selection and configuration

We select distinct new SUVs presented on U.S. manufacturer websites, then identify one exact model year, trim, powertrain, and drivetrain for each profile. We do not combine a price from one configuration with equipment from another.

The current SUV guide was researched on November 21, 2026. Because manufacturers can update prices and equipment, readers should confirm the actual vehicle’s window sticker.

Ranking factors

We consider entry and exit, seat adjustment, rearward visibility aids, parking assistance, climate and dashboard controls, cargo access, and starting MSRP. The ranking weighs how the reviewed trim’s documented equipment addresses those tasks.

We do not assign numerical scores or claim that a higher-ranked vehicle will fit every reader better. Three-row models are evaluated with particular attention to the space and access available when the third row is raised.

Prices

We report the manufacturer’s starting MSRP for the reviewed trim and identify whether destination or freight is extra. If a source does not clearly identify destination treatment, we say so rather than guessing. MSRP is not a guaranteed selling price and does not imply local inventory or a discount.

Cargo figures

When we publish a cargo number, we identify the seating row behind which it was measured and whether rear rows are folded. We omit figures that cannot be matched to the reviewed vehicle or configuration.

Road tests, fuel economy, and safety

The current guide does not claim that we road-tested the vehicles. It does not quote fuel-economy figures or safety ratings. Any future EPA figures should be checked against FuelEconomy.gov for the matching configuration; any future safety ratings should come directly from NHTSA or IIHS with the applicable model year.

Sources and corrections

Manufacturer links appear directly within each vehicle profile. Those external pages may change after our research date. Editorial corrections should be based on a verifiable source and should preserve the exact year and trim distinction.
