A calmer dealership visit

Know what to check before you arrive.

  1. Write down your non-negotiables

    Consider seat height, passenger needs, cargo behind a raised third row, garage clearance, and whether you want all-wheel drive.

  2. Ask for the reviewed configuration

    A different trim can change the seat controls, cameras, liftgate, and price. Confirm the equipment on the actual vehicle.

  3. Take the checklist with you

    Our printable checklist covers entry and exit, seat adjustment, rearward visibility, parking, dashboard controls, and cargo access.
